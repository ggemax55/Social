Freestyle drills

  • Room scan. Rap about 5 objects you can see, one per bar.
  • Rhyme ahead. Pick the rhyme word first, then build the line backwards to land on it.
  • Story mode. Who, where, what went wrong, how it ends. 16 bars.
  • Filler swap. When stuck, say the beat's count ("one, two, I'm back") instead of "uhh". Stay on time.
  • Mirror battle. Diss your own last verse. Self-roasts build punchlines fast.

Writing

Write in Armenian or English. The pad counts syllables per line and tags line endings that rhyme with the same letter, so you can see your scheme and keep bars even.

Inspiration

Your scheme
—

Syllables are estimated. Armenian often hides the ը sound in clusters (սրտում is said "sər-tum"), so add one where you hear it. The rhyme tag compares the last vowel sound and what follows it.

Keep bars evenLines in a verse should land within 2 to 3 syllables of each other at the same tempo. A long line forces you to rush.
Punchline = setup + twistBar 1 sets a normal image. Bar 2 flips a word's meaning. Put the twist word at the very end.
Show, don't tell"I was broke" is weak. "Splitting one shawarma for two days" is a picture.
Write 16, keep 8Write twice what you need, then cut the weakest half. Editing is where verses get good.

Rewrite drill

Take a flat line and upgrade it three ways: add an image, add an internal rhyme, add a double meaning.

FlatI work hard every day
ImageAlarm at five, my shadow clocks in before me
Internal rhymeGrind in the cold, mind on the gold, I never fold
Double meaningI put in work so long, my bars got overtime
Training tool

Հայերեն ռեփ

Armenian is built for rap. Stress almost always falls on the last syllable, so every line ends with a natural punch. Use that: put your strongest word last and hit it on the snare.

Last syllable winsԵրևան, Հայաստան, հաղթանակ. The stress is at the end, so line endings punch on their own. A final ը is the exception and stays unstressed.
Cheap vs. strong rhymesEndings like -ում, -ել, -ություն rhyme with thousands of words. That is easy, so the crowd won't feel it. Rhyme the root too: քար / դար / պայքար beats գնում / խոսում.
Mix carefullyYerevan slang and English words add flavor. Keep one language per punchline so the twist lands clearly.

English rap

English stress moves around inside words, so flow depends on where the strong syllables fall. Level up from one-syllable rhymes to chains of sounds that repeat across the whole bar.

Rhyme types, weakest to strongest

  • Perfect: game / name. Clean but predictable.
  • Slant: home / alone / phone / cold. Close vowels, more freedom.
  • Internal: rhymes inside the line, not only at the end.
  • Multisyllabic: "go the distance / no assistance / so persistent". Two or more syllables matching in a row.
  • Chain: one sound family carried across 4+ bars.

Fast rap

Speed is clean syllables per second, held for a long time. Build it like a gym lift: slow and perfect first, then add 5 BPM only after a clean run.

Syllable lane · follows the deck
0syllables / second
4per beat

Rap one syllable per lit box. Turn the click on in the deck to hear the grid.

The speed ladder

  1. 8ths (2 per beat) at 80 BPM. Every word clear.
  2. Triplets (3 per beat) at 80. This is the modern "Migos" bounce.
  3. 16ths (4 per beat) at 80. That is already 5.3 syllables a second.
  4. Clean run of 8 bars? Add 5 BPM. Missed words? Drop 5.
  5. Mark breaths with / in your lyrics before you start.
Clarity firstOver-pronounce consonants at slow tempo: T, K, P, Ք, Կ, Պ. At speed they soften by themselves.

Motivational rap

Motivation hits when it's true. The listener has to see your low point before they believe your comeback. Specific pain, then specific work, then the win.

Dance vibe

On the dance floor the beat is the star. Rap less, leave space, and give people a chant they can shout back after hearing it once.

TempoClub and house sit at 118 to 128 BPM. Dembow and reggaeton around 90 to 100. Afrobeats around 100 to 115.
Short phrases2 to 4 words per shout. "Hands up", "Jump", "Let's go". Long bars kill the energy.
Call and responseYou say a line, the crowd answers. Teach it in the first hook.
Give ordersDance lyrics tell bodies what to do: left, right, down, up, bounce.

Armenian dance rap

Wedding and folk dance rhythms are different from 4/4 hip-hop. Learn to count them, land key words on the big drum (դհոլ dum), and leave room for the crowd's shout: Հո՛պ!

Kochari chant · original · rhymes on -ին and -ոտ

Rapping over folk rhythm

  • Land on DUM. Put your rhyme word on the big drum hit, not between.
  • Short lines. In 2/4 a bar goes by fast. One idea per bar.
  • Speed up at the end. Armenian dances often push the tempo near the end. Tick "Speed up" in the deck to practice: +1 BPM every bar.
  • Respect the melody. When the zurna or duduk plays a phrase, stop rapping and let it breathe.
  • Producing it. Layer an 808 under the dhol's DUM, keep the tak on top, and use samples you are allowed to use.
