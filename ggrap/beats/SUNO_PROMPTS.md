# GGRap · Suno prompts

30 prompts in 3 groups. **★ = the 20 to make first** (you have about 20 downloads).
All lyrics below are original, written for GGRap. No artist names (Suno blocks them anyway).

## How to make them

1. Use Suno's **newest model**.
2. Paste the **Style of Music** text exactly. Put the **Title** in the title box.
3. **Beats (B):** turn **Instrumental ON**, leave lyrics empty.
   **Songs (S) and Soul (V):** Instrumental **OFF**, paste the lyrics.
4. Suno makes 2 versions. Listen to both and **download only the better one** (saves downloads).
5. Download as **WAV**. If your plan has **Get Stems**, download the stems too (at least **Vocals** and **Instrumental**).
6. Name the files with the ID first: `B01 Golden Hour Break.wav`, `B01 Golden Hour Break - Vocals.wav`, and so on.
7. Zip and attach them here. I measure every file (tempo, key, start point), cut loops, chop vocals, and add them to the app.

**Rights:** for the public app, make these while you have a **paid Suno plan**. Songs made on the free plan can't be used commercially, even if you upgrade later.

## What I make from them

- **Beats (B):** 4-bar loops at their exact tempo and key, matching versions for the mixer, and drum hits, 808s and melody one-shots for the beat maker.
- **Songs (S):** vocal chops (single words and syllables mapped to pads), and full chorus phrases you can drop into a beat.
- **Soul (V):** chopped soul loops, pitched choir pads and vocal ad-libs, the classic sampled hip-hop sound.
